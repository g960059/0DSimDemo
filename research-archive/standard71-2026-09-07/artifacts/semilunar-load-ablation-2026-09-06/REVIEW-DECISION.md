## Scientific points adjudicated

1. Legacy R remained while the EOA-derived quadratic coefficient increased. This warrants auditing its provenance, but does not establish definite double-counting: the two terms have different flow dependence and the historical R's fitted/physical content is not independently known.
2. Claude's use of `2r−r²` to infer >90% **static** recovery is not accepted. For the declared negligible-velocity chamber and downstream static-pressure station, kinetic head remains and static recovery is `2r(1−r)`. Astra identified this independently; the primary agent verified Reil 2022's published PRI expression and its separate Pnet/EL treatment. The source reviewer text is preserved unedited rather than silently corrected.
3. Zc moving the ventricular pressure peak earlier is a testable hypothesis, not an established cause. Continuity at a storage-node pressure peak is not proof that myocardial timing is irrelevant, and this distributed storage network is not just one two-element Windkessel.
4. C×1.5's PAP minimum reference failure is retained as data, not used to widen a gate. Neither review can turn a research counterfactual into a new healthy baseline.
5. Astra recommends an AV recovery-only arm as the next smallest mechanistic experiment: same background R, fixed 3 cm downstream geometry as an assumption, Zc=0, unchanged C/L/myocardium. This is a next research proposal, not an implemented or validated public model.
