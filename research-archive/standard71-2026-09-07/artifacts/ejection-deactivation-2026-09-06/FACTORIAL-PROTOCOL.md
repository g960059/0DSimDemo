# Factorized 2 x 2 recruitment and distortion trial

The component sensitivity separates two coupled effects. Slowing kws by 20%
delays the isometric peak by about 20 ms but reduces prescribed-shortening
work about 19%, even after peak matching. Increasing phi by 20% leaves every
isometric trace unchanged and increases shortening work about 13%, with only
a small increase in the sampled filling force. Those are counterfactual
component results, not independent physiological observations.

Before observing coupled results, test kwsScale {1, 0.8} x phiScale {1, 1.2}.
The combination keeps cs at 0.96 of control while changing recruitment and
ksu; thus it addresses the entanglement in a one-parameter kws intervention.
Retain all derived source relations, original Ca and exit60/power16. Normalize
each condition's Tref at the same isometric anchor, not its closed-loop output.
Keep HR70, TBV5050, Rsys1.05, compliance0.6, slack1.06, passive1.04, aortic L0.

No candidate is selected by gate pass count alone. Consider ET, pressure and
CI, IRT/Tei, AV loss, waveform and filling pressure jointly. If a direction
cannot improve the ET/flow tradeoff, stop that direction without a large grid.
Only a genuinely useful condition receives bilateral reserve and 1 ms checks.
Published baseline, numeric cutoffs, and public control domains are unchanged.
