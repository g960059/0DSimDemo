# Existing deactivation-path ablation

Research only. Original ventricular calcium, current kuw/kws/Aeff/nTm, valve
laws and the published model remain unchanged. No afterload protocol is run.

The question is whether changing the existing state-free S-to-U excess-bridge
exit can extend useful force during ejection without increasing residual active
force during filling. This is a constitutive counterfactual, not a source fit.
Reference Land stretch is 1.06, an exploratory lead rather than measured truth.

Before closed-loop results: compare no exit and rate/power pairs
15/16, 30/16, 60/16 (control), 120/16, 30/8, 60/8, 120/8. These are bounded
factor contrasts inside the existing extension domain, not clinical ranges.
Use periodic isometric trials at actual Land stretch 1.0, 1.1, 1.166, 1.195 and
replay the accepted 1 ms center/high-volume shortening and calcium histories.
Normalize Tref linearly to the control's isometric peak at stretch 1.166; report
other lengths and dynamic histories without renormalizing them individually.
Retain the declared research Tref domain; flag, do not clamp, any violation.

Record force width and relaxation, late-ejection work/force, phase-0.7 active
pressure, mean phase-0.6–0.9 active pressure, and the exit flux. Verify that the
unmodified replay reproduces its accepted trajectory and that fixed-input
equilibrium is preserved. Prescribed shortening is not a closed-loop prediction.

Advance at most four distinct initial conditions to the closed loop: control,
one moderate slowing and (if component differences are small or interpretation
requires it) one stronger/no-exit contrast, plus an opposite-direction control.
Selection is explicit exploratory mechanism discrimination, not an automatic
new gate. Existing timing, gradient, wave-shape and reserve checks remain visible.
Do not relax a cutoff because a candidate is close. Only promising coupled
results merit bilateral reserve and refined-step confirmation.
