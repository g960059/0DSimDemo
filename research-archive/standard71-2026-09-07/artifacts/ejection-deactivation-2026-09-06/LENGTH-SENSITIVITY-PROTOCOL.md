# Anchored length-sensitivity counterfactual

Land 2017 Eq29 uses a linear CaT50–length relation. Section3.3 fits its slope
to five skinned cells at three sarcomere lengths. Sections3.5–3.6 transfer to
intact muscle/organ with different calcium sensitivity, cooperativity and
kinetics; the paper explicitly lacks paired intact force/length/calcium data.
This is an uncertainty in model transfer, not evidence that the original slope
is erroneous. Do not claim the following contrasts are measured normal ranges.

At fixed original calcium and current reference stretch1.06, compare beta1
scales 1, 0.8, 0.6, tying CaT50Ref so CaT50 at actual Land stretch1.166 stays
0.4066 uM. This preserves the entire isometric response at the anchor, rather
than optimizing an additional calcium-affinity and amplitude coordinate.
All other primitive/derived kinetic values, beta0, Tref219054.6604885131Pa,
exit60/power16, and state schemas remain unchanged. CaT50 remains positive
over the modeled length domain with the existing cap1.2.

Compare four isometric lengths and the same prescribed center/high trajectories.
Determine whether shortening-induced deactivation, rather than just twitch
duration, can change ejection force while limiting high-length residual force.
First distinguish this finite mechanistic contrast from an identifiable
calibration; source fit errors/covariance are unavailable. If advancing, run
only these three closed-loop conditions before deciding on reserve/fine work.
This does not admit source-Land parameters as ordinary baseline/preset knobs.
