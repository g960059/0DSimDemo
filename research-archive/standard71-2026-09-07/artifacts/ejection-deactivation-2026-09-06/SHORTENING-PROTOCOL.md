# Separate recruitment from shortening distortion

The exit-rate ablation did not provide useful prolongation: rate30 increased
ET by 6 ms but IRT by 26 ms; no-exit made the E-wave observation unavailable.
No further reserve or fine-grid run is justified for that family.

Next perform component-only, one-factor +/-20% kws or phi and +/-10% Aeff
contrasts, all with original calcium and exit60/power16. Phi and Aeff affect
distortion dynamics but not isometric population dynamics; kws also changes
recruitment, ksu and cs. Derived coefficients must follow the source formulas.
These contrasts are engineering sensitivity intervals, not normal-human ranges
or a new baseline fitting domain. The source data do not uniquely identify
whole-organ twitch, shortening, calcium and reference length together.

Use the same four actual stretches, recorded center/high-volume histories and
single-point peak normalization as the earlier component trial. Examine a
tradeoff table before admitting any new closed-loop construction. No new state,
independent LV/RV family, source-Ca waveform or pressure gain is introduced.
