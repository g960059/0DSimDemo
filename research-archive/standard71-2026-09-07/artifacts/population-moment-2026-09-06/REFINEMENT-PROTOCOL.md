# Follow-up before v2 execution

v1 exposed negative active stress in the slow-rate moment model on the recorded
LVFW path, despite positive force in all 2% ramps. Add 10% ramps using the same
20/100ms durations, Ca and length conditions, without changing the kernel or
parameters. The 20ms/10% ramp is a deliberately severe constitutive probe, NOT a
normality condition. The 100ms/10% ramp overlaps the scale of shortening protocols
discussed in Land 2017 §3.4, but uses intact-model parameters; it is not a direct
fit to skinned-cell experiments. Preserve v1 as exploratory provenance.

Compare 2/1/0.5ms pointwise (normalized by the fine-grid peak, not by near-zero
stress), work, sign and tails. Refine only if the qualitative conclusion changes.
Do not interpret negative crossbridge stress alone as proof of numerical failure
or disease: transient resistance during imposed shortening can produce it.
Its presence in a selected old trajectory does, however, preclude claiming that
this substitution is already a validated drop-in normal material.
