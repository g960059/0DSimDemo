# Source population / distortion recovery separation

Prospective follow-up after closed-loop-v1. All four Ca/length contrasts were
settled but failed timing/gradient criteria. Faster Ca shortened ET further.

The source Land law uses cs = phi * ksu (for the pinned source duty ratios).
Slowing kws to hold ejection also slows removal of S and distortion recovery.
For a fixed-length, no-influx approximation with -1 < zetaS < 0, no added exit,
the strong-force derivative is proportional to

    S * [-ksu * (1 + zetaS) - cs * zetaS].

It can be positive despite falling S if zetaS < -1/(1 + phi). This is a
limiting-case explanation of rebound in the component assay, NOT a sufficient
criterion for whole-heart pressure shape. W/influx, changing length, geometry,
passive stress and loading have been omitted in this approximation.
