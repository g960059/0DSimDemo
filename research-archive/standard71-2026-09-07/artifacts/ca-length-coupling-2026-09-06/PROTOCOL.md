## Source interpretation

The current alpha anchor was fitted to the digitized Ca curve, not raw source
data. The publication combines Ca and muscle measurements from different
preparations. The quoted final source twitch timings are TTP175, RT50 121,
RT95 281ms; RT95 is not RT90, and none is organ IRT. Figure 6 includes
length-dependent twitch responses. Whole-organ loading/filling in the paper
is not this closed-loop circulation. A qualitative figure match is not a
normal-human baseline validation.
